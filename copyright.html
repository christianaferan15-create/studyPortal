Copyright & DMCA Policy

Study Portal respects intellectual property rights. Learn how we handle student uploads and how copyright owners can request material removal.

1. DMCA & Safe Harbor Compliance

Study Portal operates as an educational content hosting provider under standard Safe Harbor and DMCA framework guidelines. Content on this platform is uploaded by registered students across various Nigerian institutions for peer review and exam preparation.

We do not proactively produce or claim copyright over third-party lecture notes, past exam questions, or student summaries. However, we strictly forbid the unauthorized sharing of copyrighted commercial books or proprietary institutional materials.

2. Repeat Infringer Policy

In accordance with digital copyright regulations, Study Portal maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy for repeat copyright violations:

  • First Notice: Flagged content is immediately hidden or removed, and the uploader receives a formal warning.
  • Second Notice: Upload privileges for the associated user account are suspended pending investigation.
  • Third Notice: The user account is permanently terminated and banned from uploading future materials.
